# Seeking: K-12 ND Student to Try Our Hackathon Project (15 min, async)

Hey everyone! We're building **TaskQuest** for Track 1 (AI for K-12 Learners) — an AI tool that breaks down overwhelming schoolwork into tiny, game-ified steps with a pet companion.

**We need 1–2 neurodivergent K-12 students** (ADHD, dyslexia, autism — any ND) to try it for 10–15 minutes and give honest feedback. This is a submission requirement and we want to get it right.

## What It Involves
- 🖥️ Open a website (no signup, no download)
- 🗣️ Type or say one task (e.g. "read 20 pages" or "finish my math homework")
- 👆 Click through the steps, try completing a couple
- 💬 Tell us: what felt good? what was confusing? what would make you want to come back?

## Logistics
- **Async** — you try it whenever works, send feedback however is easiest (voice message, DM, short video, parent relays it — anything)
- **~15 minutes** total
- **Privacy**: no data leaves the browser. Nothing is recorded unless you choose to.
- **Parent/guardian**: we're happy to explain the project to them first

## Who We Are
Solo hacker, ADHD myself. Built this because I know what it feels like to stare at "write a book report" for three hours. I want to make sure it actually helps a real kid, not just my idea of one.

Reply here or DM me — happy to answer any questions! 🙏
