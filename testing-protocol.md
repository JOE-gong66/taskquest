# TaskQuest K-12 Testing Protocol (15 min, async)

## For the Student

### Step 1: Open (30 sec)
Go to **https://lovely-conkies-70714e.netlify.app/** on any device (phone/tablet/laptop all work).

### Step 2: Start a Quest (2 min)
- If you can type: type a task you actually need to do. Like "read chapter 3" or "start my science project".
- If typing is hard: click the 🎤 button and say your task out loud.
- Click "Questify!"

### Step 3: Try the Steps (5 min)
- Look at the steps the AI created. Do they make sense? Are they small enough?
- Try completing the first step (click it).
- If one step still feels too big, click "🔍 Too big".
- Try the "💬 Coach" button on any step.

### Step 4: Meet Your Pet (2 min)
- After completing your first step, a pet modal should appear.
- Pick a pet, name it, confirm.
- Click the pet. What does it say?

### Step 5: Explore (optional)
- Try clicking the 🅰️ button in the top bar (font toggle).
- Try the 🔊 button (sound toggle).
- Try the "🔎 Just the next step" button.

---

## Questions to Ask After (pick 3–5, whichever feel natural)

1. **What was the best part?** What made you smile or feel good?

2. **What was confusing or annoying?** Anything you didn't understand?

3. **Did the AI steps feel right?** Were they small enough to actually start? Was the first step easy enough?

4. **Would you come back tomorrow?** Why or why not?

5. **What would you tell a friend about this?**

6. **Was anything too baby-ish?** Or too hard to understand?

7. **If you could add one thing, what would it be?**
